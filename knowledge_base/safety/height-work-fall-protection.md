# 概述

Any work at or above 2 meters from the fall reference plane is treated as height work. Safety harnesses must be attached to an anchorage point capable of minimum 15kN with lanyard secured at body level or above. Scaffolds require joint inspection and tagging by technical lead, safety officer, and erection foreman before use.

# 细节

The 15kN anchorage criterion is based on the energy absorption requirements of personal fall arrest systems. Joint scaffold acceptance ensures structural integrity, proper tie-offs, and compliance with design. The 'high anchor, low use' principle minimizes free-fall distance.

# 示例

Fall threshold: 2m. Harness anchorage: ≥15kN. Scaffold acceptance: technical responsible, safety officer, erection foreman sign-off.

# 注意事项

Inspect harnesses before each use; avoid swinging fall hazards; scaffolds must be re-inspected after weather events.
