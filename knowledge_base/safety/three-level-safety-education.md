# 概述

All personnel must pass a sequential three-level safety education (company, project, team) with prescribed minimum training hours and examinations to ensure baseline safety competence.

# 细节

The company-level training (≥24 hours) covers general HSE policies and legal requirements. The project-level training (≥16 hours) focuses on site-specific hazards and control measures. The team-level training (≥8 hours) addresses task-specific risks and safe work procedures. Only after passing each level and final assessment can personnel work. Special operation personnel must hold valid certificates and be verified as the same person.

# 示例

Company-level 24 hours; Project-level 16 hours; Team-level 8 hours. Special operation personnel: human-certificate matching enforced.

# 参考

Petrochemical Construction Safety Technical Specification (GB 50484-2019); Company QHSE management system

# 注意事项

No waivers or shortcuts for experienced hires; failure in any exam requires retraining.
