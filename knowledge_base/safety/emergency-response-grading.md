# 概述

Level I (fatality or ≥3 serious injuries) activates company-wide emergency plan and requires reporting to group headquarters and local emergency management within 30 minutes. Level II (serious injury or equipment loss ≥¥500k) activates project-level plan and reports to company within 2 hours. Level III (minor injury or general equipment fault) is handled on-site and reported within 24 hours.

# 细节

This tiered system aligns resource mobilization with severity, preventing both over-reaction and under-reaction. The strict time limits ensure swift decision-making and compliance with regulatory requirements.

# 示例

Level I: 30 min to group and authority; Level II: 2 hr to company; Level III: 24 hr report. Financial threshold: ≥¥500k for Level II.

# 注意事项

Definition of 'serious injury' must be clear (refer to GB/T 6441). Response times depend on communication availability; redundant notification channels needed.
