# 概述

All incidents are investigated under the 'Four Not Let Go' discipline: not releasing until causes are clear, responsible persons are dealt with, corrective actions are implemented, and relevant personnel are educated. The investigation report, due within 7 working days, must include direct cause analysis, root cause analysis (5-Why method), and corrective action plan with deadlines.

# 细节

This approach ensures accountability and learning. The 5-Why method digs beyond immediate errors to systemic failures. The 7-day deadline enforces timeliness while still allowing thorough investigation. The requirement to educate relevant personnel turns each incident into a training opportunity.

# 示例

Report format: direct cause, root cause via 5-Why, corrective actions with completion dates. Due: 7 working days after incident.

# 参考

Company QHSE incident management procedure; GB/T 6441

# 注意事项

5-Why can be subjective; supplement with event-and-causal-factor charting for complex incidents. Legal privilege considerations when communicating externally.
