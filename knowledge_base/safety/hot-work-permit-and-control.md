# 概述

All hot work is subject to a graded permit system: Level I (highest risk) approved by Project Manager, Level II by HSE Manager, Level III by Construction Team Leader. Each hot work point is equipped with at least two 8kg dry powder extinguishers and a dedicated fire watch.

# 细节

The 'Ten Prohibitions of Hot Work' rule is mandatory. Outside high-altitude hot work is prohibited when wind exceeds Force 5, and all outside hot work prohibited above Force 6. This graduated authorization ensures that higher-risk tasks receive more stringent review while minimizing bureaucratic delay for low-risk tasks.

# 示例

Level I: project manager approval; Level II: HSE manager; Level III: construction team leader. Extinguishers: 2 x 8kg minimum. Wind limits: Force 5 for high-altitude, Force 6 for all outside.

# 注意事项

Wind-speed monitoring must be continuous on site; portable anemometers should be calibrated regularly. Permit expiry and shift change re-validation required.
