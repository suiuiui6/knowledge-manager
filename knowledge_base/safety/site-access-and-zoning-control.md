# 概述

The construction site is fully enclosed with face-recognition turnstiles. Four functional zones (construction, prefabrication, office, living) are physically isolated, and high-risk areas are additionally protected by electronic fences and infrared alarms.

# 细节

This approach prevents uncontrolled entry, mixing of personnel with different risk exposures, and unmonitored access to critical hazard zones. High-risk areas such as height work, confined space, and hot work zones are marked by separate electronic fences with infrared intrusion detection to provide immediate alert if boundaries are breached.

# 示例

Face-recognition turnstiles at main gate; independent electronic fences around confined space entry points; infrared beams at scaffold edges.

# 参考

GB 50484-2019; Company QHSE site management procedure

# 注意事项

False alarms may occur in adverse weather; regular testing and cleaning of cameras/sensors required.
