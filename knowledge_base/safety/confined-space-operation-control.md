# 概述

Before entry, mechanical ventilation is verified and atmospheric testing is performed within 30 minutes prior. Oxygen content must be 19.5-23.5%, combustible gas below 10% LEL, H₂S <10mg/m³, CO <30mg/m³. During work, re-test every 2 hours and assign at least two attendants per team.

# 细节

The sequence eliminates the risk of entering a hazardous atmosphere. The 30-minute pre-test window ensures conditions haven't changed. Continuous monitoring and frequent re-testing (2-hour intervals) catches gradual changes. Dual attendant requirement ensures one can summon help while the other observes.

# 示例

Gas limits: O₂ 19.5-23.5%, combustible gas <10% LEL, H₂S <10mg/m³, CO <30mg/m³. Re-test every 2 hours; 2+ attendants per shift.

# 注意事项

Calibration of gas detectors before each use; must account for potential gas layering; backup rescue plan and equipment essential.
