# 概述

Each construction zone must have an emergency materials point stocked with: first-aid kit (tourniquet, burn cream, eye wash), 2 positive-pressure SCBA sets, 10 gas masks, emergency lights, and a stretcher. The HSE department inspects and refills all points on the 1st of every month.

# 细节

Standardizing the list guarantees a minimum capability for on-site first response. Monthly fixed-date inspections create a routine that is easy to audit and prevents stockouts. The inclusion of SCBA and gas masks reflects the primary risks of petrochemical sites.

# 示例

First-aid kit contents: tourniquet, burn cream, eye wash. SCBA: 2 sets. Gas masks: 10. Stretcher: 1. Monthly inspection: every 1st day.

# 参考

Company QHSE emergency supplies standard; GB 50484-2019

# 注意事项

Check expiry dates of medical supplies and pharmaceuticals. SCBA cylinders require periodic hydrostatic testing. Ensure personnel stationed know how to use equipment.
